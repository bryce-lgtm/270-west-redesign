Situations

When veterans come to us for reassessments

Reassessments are common. Your health changes over time, and your rating should reflect that.

Situation 01

"My condition has gotten worse."

If you have new medical evidence, a reassessment may be the right next step. We help you decide.

Situation 02

"I have a new diagnosis related to my service."

New conditions sometimes warrant a new claim rather than a reassessment. We help you tell the difference.

Situation 03

"I'm not sure it's worth the paperwork."

We do a quick, honest review and tell you if it is.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does 270 West do?

We help veterans and their families understand and navigate the disability benefits process: understanding your options, organizing the information a claim needs, preparing and filing documentation, connecting you with medical professionals and resources, and keeping you informed about what comes next.

How much does 270 West charge?

Our fee for benefit navigation and filing is 10%, and we only get paid if your claim is successful. There is no fee for an initial conversation, and we explain our fees and terms clearly before you decide whether to move forward.

Does 270 West charge for everything it does?

No. Many of the supports and resources we provide are at no charge. The service we charge for is benefit navigation and filing.

Is there any commitment required for the first meeting?

No. The first meeting is a conversation. We listen, answer questions and explain where we may be able to help. Then you decide whether there is a fit.

Does 270 West take over my claim?

No, and that distinction matters. We take on the administrative work, organization and follow-up, but it remains your claim. You review the information, ask questions and approve what is submitted.

Can 270 West guarantee my claim will be approved?

No responsible organization can guarantee how VAC will decide a claim. What we can promise is that we take your experience seriously, prepare the information carefully and support you through the process.

Is 270 West part of Veterans Affairs Canada?

No. We are an independent advisory service based in Canada and are not affiliated with Veterans Affairs Canada.

Wondering if a reassessment is worth it?

We'll do an honest review, free of charge, and tell you what we think.
