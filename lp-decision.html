We help you understand your VAC decision

A decision you disagree with can be difficult to receive. You deserve time to ask questions, understand the reasons and consider your next steps. You do not have to work out your review or appeal by yourself.

Situation 01

“I received a decision I disagree with and I do not know what to do next.”

We go through the decision with you, explain the available review routes and help identify where additional information, medical assessment or other supporting evidence may be useful.

Situation 02

“My assessment seems low. What route should I look at?”

We look at whether the issue is the decision itself, the assessment level or a change in your condition, and explain whether a Departmental Review, reassessment or another route may fit.

Situation 03

“I have new information that was not considered in the original decision.”

New supporting information may be relevant to a Departmental Review. We help you understand what VAC decided, identify the information that may fill a gap and prepare the supporting material with you.

For VAC disability benefit decisions, there is no time limit to request a review of the Department’s decision. VAC and VRAB make their own decisions, so we cannot promise a result. 270 West Consulting is independent and is not affiliated with Veterans Affairs Canada or VRAB.

We will review your decision with you

A review or appeal is not a fight. It is a structured opportunity to look again at a decision with the right supporting information. We start with the decision, identify the gaps and help you understand whether the next route is a VAC departmental review or a VRAB proceeding.

  1. 01
    Understand the decision
    We review the decision letter with you and identify what VAC decided and why.
  2. 02
    Build the supporting information
    We help you gather new evidence, coordinate a medical assessment where appropriate and research relevant precedent for a Departmental Review.
  3. 03
    Prepare for a VRAB Review Hearing, if needed
    If the matter proceeds to VRAB, free legal representation is available through the Bureau of Pensions Advocates. Your legal representative determines what additional evidence is needed, and we can help you gather that information.
  4. 04
    Support the next step
    If the VRAB Review decision is unfavourable, you and your legal representative may proceed to an Appeal Hearing. We remain available to help with supporting information requested by counsel.
