Ways to get help with VAC benefits

Choose the level of VAC benefits support that fits you.

Some veterans manage their VAC applications themselves. Others use free sources of support. Some prefer a dedicated, fee-based navigation service to help coordinate the details.

There is no single right route. The right choice depends on your situation, your time, your comfort with the process and the level of support you want.

Compare your options → Talk through your options

What is veteran benefits navigation?

Veteran benefits navigation is hands-on support that helps a veteran understand the routes available, bring together the information a claim may need, coordinate practical steps and make sense of what comes next.

The navigator does not decide the claim. VAC does. The veteran remains in control of the information they provide and the decisions they make.

Three ways veterans often get help

  • Manage it yourself

    What it can look like

    You research VAC programs, organize records and medical information, complete forms, arrange appointments and track follow-up directly.

    May fit if…

    You are comfortable with the process, have the time and energy to manage the details, and know where to go when questions arise.

    No service fee.

  • Use free support

    What it can look like

    Veteran-serving organizations and public resources may provide information, claim support or representation depending on the organization and stage of the process.

    May fit if…

    You want help without a service fee and the available scope, timing and level of involvement meet your needs.

    No service fee.

  • Choose dedicated fee-based navigation

    What it can look like

    A dedicated navigator works with you one-to-one, helps organize the route and information, coordinates practical steps and stays involved through agreed stages of the process.

    May fit if…

    You want more hands-on coordination, continuity or help managing multiple moving pieces rather than carrying the process yourself.

    Fees vary by provider; understand the terms before you proceed.

Free support can be the right choice.

Canada has veteran-serving organizations and public programs that provide valuable support at no charge. For many veterans, those services are exactly what they need.

A fee-based service is not a replacement for the veteran-support community. It is another option for people who want a different level of dedicated coordination, continuity or practical involvement.

When fee-based navigation may be worth considering

A dedicated navigation service may be useful when:

  • You have limited time or energy to research, organize and follow up on every step.
  • Your situation involves multiple conditions, applications, previous decisions or different possible routes.
  • You want one point of contact to help keep appointments, documentation and next steps organized.
  • You need help finding experienced health-care professionals for an assessment or documentation.
  • You have started the process but are unsure what is outstanding or what should happen next.
  • You simply prefer experienced, hands-on help even if you could manage the process yourself.

None of these automatically means you need a paid service. They are reasons to compare the level of support available and decide what is worth it to you.

Know what you are choosing.

Before choosing any support — free or paid — ask:

  • What exactly will you do, and what will I still need to do?
  • Who reviews and approves the information before it goes to VAC?
  • What experience do you have with situations like mine?
  • How do you handle medical assessments and documentation?
  • How will you communicate with me and track outstanding items?
  • What happens after VAC makes a decision?
  • Are there fees? If so, when do they apply and what do they cover?
  • Can I continue working with other veteran-serving organizations or professionals?

Dedicated, hands-on benefits navigation

270 West is an independent Canadian company providing dedicated veteran benefits navigation. We work alongside the broader veteran-support ecosystem and respect the free options available to veterans.

Our role is to bring experience, coordination and continuity to the moving pieces around a claim: understanding your circumstances, research and preparation, medical connections and appointments where appropriate, documentation, follow-up, helping you file, and explaining VAC updates and decisions.

Conversations with our team are always free. If you choose to work with us on a claim, our fee is 10% + HST on a successful claim. You only pay if the claim is successful, and we explain the service, fee and terms before you decide to proceed.
