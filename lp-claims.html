Experience and support at every step

From your first conversation through your claim and decision, we help make a detailed process more manageable. Our experience helps us know what questions to ask and how to bring the pieces together, while you stay informed and in control.

  1. 01
    Start with a conversation
    Tell us about your service, your health and what you are trying to resolve. We will listen, ask questions and draw on experience with similar trades and service circumstances to understand what may need a closer look.
  2. 02
    Build a roadmap together
    We will map the route, information, appointments and next steps that may apply to your situation, so you can see what needs to happen and in what order.
  3. 03
    We coordinate the details
    We help bring the right information together, connect you with experienced medical professionals where appropriate, coordinate appointments and help with practical details such as transportation when needed.
  4. 04
    We stay in your corner
    We remain involved while VAC reviews your claim, help you understand the decision and talk through next steps as needed, including other benefits and supports that may be relevant afterward.
