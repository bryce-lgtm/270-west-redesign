How we support you through the VAC benefits process

From your first conversation through your application and decision, we provide practical help and keep you informed about what comes next.

  1. 01
    Start with a free conversation
    Tell us about your service, your situation, and where you are in the process. We’ll listen and talk through how we can help.
  2. 02
    Build a plan together
    We’ll identify the next steps for your situation and explain what to expect.
  3. 03
    We get to work
    We’ll help you prepare your application and coordinate relevant appointments and information along the way.
  4. 04
    We stay in your corner
    We’ll stay involved while VAC reviews your application, help you understand the decision, and talk through next steps if needed.
