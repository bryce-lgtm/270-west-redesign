VAC Reviews & Appeals support

If you disagree with a VAC disability benefits decision, there are different review routes. We help you understand the decision, gather supporting information for a Departmental Review where appropriate, and help you prepare for the next stage if the matter proceeds to the Veterans Review and Appeal Board (VRAB).

Situations

When veterans come to us for VAC Reviews & Appeals help

Situation 01

“I received a decision I disagree with and I do not know what to do next.”

We go through the decision with you, explain the available review routes and help identify where additional information, medical assessment or other supporting evidence may be useful.

Situation 02

“My assessment seems low. What route should I look at?”

We look at whether the issue is the decision itself, the assessment level or a change in your condition, and explain whether a Departmental Review, reassessment or another route may fit.

Situation 03

“I have new information that was not considered in the original decision.”

New supporting information may be relevant to a Departmental Review. We help you understand what VAC decided, identify the information that may fill a gap and prepare the supporting material with you.

Our approach

How we help with VAC Reviews & Appeals

A review or appeal is not a fight. It is a structured opportunity to look again at a decision with the right supporting information. We start with the decision, identify the gaps and help you understand whether the next route is a VAC departmental review or a VRAB proceeding.

01
Understand the decision and the route
We review the decision with you and explain whether a VAC departmental review or a VRAB route may apply.
02
Fill the gaps
We help identify missing or unclear supporting information, connect you with experienced medical professionals where appropriate and research relevant precedent and context.
03
Prepare for a VAC departmental review
Where a departmental review is the next route, we help you prepare new supporting evidence and a clear package for you to send to VAC. If the decision remains unchanged, much of that work may help support the next stage.
04
Support the transition to VRAB when needed
Free legal advice and representation are available through the Bureau of Pensions Advocates. Your lawyer determines what additional evidence or support is needed for the VRAB matter; where appropriate, we help you gather that information and stay coordinated with the process.

Questions about VAC Reviews & Appeals

Is a departmental review the same as a Veterans Review and Appeal Board (VRAB) appeal?

No. VAC conducts a departmental review of its own decision, often considering new information. VRAB is an independent tribunal. Its first stage is a review hearing. An appeal hearing may follow if you disagree with the Board’s review decision.

Can I seek a review if VAC approved my claim but I disagree with the assessment?

Yes, a disability assessment can be reviewed or appealed. If your condition has worsened since the assessment, you may instead need a reassessment. The distinction depends on what has changed and which decision you want examined.

Is it too late to seek a review or appeal of my disability benefits decision?

No. For VAC disability benefit decisions, there is no time limit to request a review of the Department’s decision. If the matter proceeds to VRAB, the first Board stage is a Review Hearing, followed by an Appeal Hearing if you disagree with the Review decision. Bring the decision letters you have and we can help you understand which route you are at.

Do I need a lawyer for a VRAB hearing?

Veterans have access to free legal representation through the Bureau of Pensions Advocates for VRAB hearings. A BPA lawyer can advise you, prepare the case and represent you before the Board. Other representation options exist, but the free BPA service is the primary option to explain first. 270 West can help you gather supporting information requested for the case.
