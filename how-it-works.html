How VAC claims work and how we support you

A VAC claim moves through preparation, submission, VAC’s review and a written decision. We agree on a plan, prepare the application, and bring it to you for review and approval before submitting it. We handle follow-up and talk through VAC’s decision and next steps with you.

Our process

How we work through your VAC claim

These steps describe our work on a new disability claim. If you have already started an application or received a decision, we begin at the point that applies to you.

01

We listen first

In a free 30-minute conversation, we learn about your service, your health and any contact you have already had with VAC. You can tell us what you are trying to do or where you have become stuck. You do not need to arrive with a complete set of documents.

02

We identify the right route

We discuss which VAC benefits may be relevant and whether your situation calls for a new claim, a review or appeal, a reassessment, or another kind of support. We explain the role we could play and other available support options. You decide whether to work with us.

03

We plan the information your claim needs

We identify the details you can provide, any medical information that may be needed and the records VAC obtains directly. Where appropriate, we help coordinate appointments and keep track of what is outstanding. Health professionals provide their own independent assessments.

04

We prepare the application for your review

We draft the application with your input and go through it together. You can ask questions, correct details and make sure your experience is represented accurately. We don’t submit a claim until you have reviewed and approved it.

05

We help submit and follow up

After your approval, we help file the claim. VAC reviews the application and may request further information. We help you understand and respond to those requests, and keep you informed about anything that needs your attention.

06

We go through the decision with you

When VAC issues its decision, we explain the letter and discuss the options relevant to your situation. That may mean understanding an approved benefit, considering a review or appeal, or looking at other support and benefits you or your family may need afterward.

Fees and timing: know the terms before you decide

Our fees

The first conversation is free and does not commit you to using our services. Our fee for benefit navigation and filing is 10%, and we only get paid if your claim is successful. We explain the terms before you decide to proceed.

Many other supports and resources we provide are available at no charge.

The time involved

The time needed to prepare a claim depends on your circumstances and the information required. VAC’s review time also varies. We explain the steps within our control and keep you informed when VAC needs something further. We cannot guarantee when VAC will decide a claim or what the decision will be.

270 West Consulting is independent and is not affiliated with Veterans Affairs Canada. VAC makes decisions about eligibility and benefits.

Common questions

Questions about working with us

Do I need to use the online status checker first?

No. You can book a conversation directly. The short online form is an optional starting point.

What if I have already started my VAC claim application?

We look at what you have completed and what remains. Then we explain where our help could be useful. You do not have to start over simply because you contacted us partway through.

What if I have already received a VAC decision?

Bring the decision letter to a conversation with us. We can help you understand what VAC decided and discuss the routes that may still be available.

Can I apply on my own or use free support?

Yes. You can apply directly through VAC, and other organizations offer support. Our service is an option if you want more hands-on help with preparation, coordination and follow-up.

Can someone in my family take part?

Yes. A person you trust can join the conversation. We confirm your permission before discussing your personal case with them or acting on your behalf.

Can I keep working with other organizations or professionals?

Absolutely. We respect the support you already have and, with your permission, can coordinate with others where helpful.

Can you communicate with VAC for me?

With the appropriate authorization, we can help prepare questions and assist with follow-up on your VAC claims. We keep you informed and involved in decisions about your claim.

Is 270 West against VAC?

No. VAC administers the benefits system and makes the decisions. We help veterans work through that system with clear information and practical support.
