Components of a strong claim

Components of strong VAC claims

Strong Veterans Affairs claims contain a detailed service history, complete documentation of a current condition, and a credible connection between the two. We help you demonstrate all three.

  1. 01

    Detailed service history

    Relevant details about your service and the events or experiences connected with your claim.

  2. 02

    Complete documentation

    A current diagnosis and medical records that identify and describe your condition.

  3. 03

    Credible connections

    An explanation of how your condition relates to your service. We help make sure your account is clearly presented in the application.

Common questions

Questions about filing a VAC claim

Can I talk to 270 West before I have a diagnosis?

Yes. We can discuss your situation and what information an application may need. VAC requires a diagnosed condition to determine entitlement to disability benefits, and a health-care professional provides the medical assessment.

What is an applicant statement?

It is your opportunity to explain how your condition relates to your service. We help you present the relevant details clearly, and you review the statement before submission.

Who completes a VAC medical questionnaire?

A doctor or other health-care professional completes and signs the relevant questionnaire. We help you understand where it fits in the application and what you need to follow up on.

Do I need to request my Canadian Armed Forces service health records?

VAC requests those records directly from the Department of National Defence or Library and Archives Canada. We can help you identify other information that may be useful to include.

Can I apply for more than one condition?

Yes. VAC recommends a separate account of each condition in the application. We help keep the details for each condition clear.
