270 West Consulting accessibility statement

Built for everyone.

We're committed to making this site usable for veterans and family members of all abilities. Here's our current accessibility status and how to reach us if you encounter a barrier.

Effective: April 2026

Our commitment

We're committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, in line with WCAG 2.1 AA where feasible.

Current status

We continually review and improve the site's accessibility. Recent improvements include keyboard navigation, alt text on key images, and high-contrast typography.

Known limitations

We're aware of areas that need work, including some PDF resources without full screen-reader markup. We're addressing these as we update content.

Need help or found a barrier?

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in an alternative format, please email accessibility@270westconsulting.ca. We aim to respond within two business days.
