How we support you through the VAC benefits process

From your first conversation through your application and decision, we provide practical help and keep you informed about what comes next.

  1. 01
    Start with a free conversation
    Tell us about your service, your situation, and where you are in the process. We’ll listen and talk through how we can help.
  2. 02
    Build a plan together
    We’ll identify the next steps for your situation and explain what to expect.
  3. 03
    We get to work
    We’ll help prepare your application and coordinate relevant appointments and information along the way.
  4. 04
    We stay in your corner
    We’ll stay involved while VAC reviews your application, help you understand the decision, and talk through next steps if needed.

Hands-on help with claims, appeals, and reassessments

You may be starting an application, reviewing a decision, or dealing with a condition that has changed. We work with you on the next steps for your situation.

01

VAC claims

We help prepare your disability benefits application, coordinate medical questionnaires with a health-care provider, and keep track of what needs to be submitted.

02

VAC appeals

VAC reassessment

Our promise to veterans

Count on having someone in your corner.

Veteran Understanding

Our roots are in a family of veterans. We listen to your experience, respect your priorities, and explain the Veterans Affairs Canada benefits process without making assumptions about what you need.

Active Involvement

We take on the work of preparing your application, coordinating appointments, and tracking next steps. You stay informed and choose how to proceed.

Connected Support

With your permission, we coordinate with health-care providers and involve family or caregivers. We also provide other veteran-serving resources and supports and help you work through the steps after VAC’s decision.
