Hands-on help with claims, reviews and appeals, and reassessments

You may be starting a claim, reviewing a decision or dealing with a condition that has changed. We work with you on the next steps for your situation.

01

VAC Claims

We help prepare your disability benefits application, coordinate medical questionnaires with experienced health-care professionals and keep track of the information needed before you file with VAC.

02

VAC Reviews & Appeals

If you disagree with a VAC decision, we help you understand the route that applies, identify information that may strengthen the file and prepare the supporting material with you.

03

VAC Reassessments

If a condition VAC has already recognized has worsened, we help you understand whether a reassessment is the right route, connect you with an appropriate medical professional for an updated assessment and prepare the reassessment paperwork with you.

04

Ongoing Support

Support continues beyond the claim and the decision. We help veterans and families understand related VAC benefits, treatment supports, allowances and caregiver programs, and connect with practical and community resources when needs change.

How we help you navigate VAC benefits

From your first conversation through your claim and decision, we help make a detailed process more manageable. Our experience helps us know what questions to ask and how to bring the pieces together, while you stay informed and in control.

  1. 01
    Start with a conversation
    Tell us about your service, your health and what you are trying to resolve. We will listen, ask questions and draw on experience with similar trades and service circumstances to understand what may need a closer look.
  2. 02
    Build a roadmap together
    We will map the route, information, appointments and next steps that may apply to your situation, so you can see what needs to happen and in what order.
  3. 03
    We coordinate the details
    We help bring the right information together, connect you with experienced medical professionals where appropriate, coordinate appointments and help with practical details such as transportation when needed.
  4. 04
    We stay in your corner
    We remain involved while VAC reviews your claim, help you understand the decision and talk through next steps as needed, including other benefits and supports that may be relevant afterward.
