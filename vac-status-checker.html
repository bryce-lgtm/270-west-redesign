Frequently asked questions

Who can qualify for VAC benefits?

VAC benefits may be available to serving or former Canadian Armed Forces members, eligible RCMP members and, for some programs, family members or survivors. For disability benefits, VAC generally looks at whether there is a diagnosed condition and a connection between that condition and service. Eligibility varies by program and type of service. We can help you understand which programs may be relevant; VAC makes the eligibility decision.

What benefits am I eligible for?

Based on your service and what you share with us, a 270 West team member can discuss VAC benefits and supports that may be relevant and explain what each one generally requires. The online checker is a starting point for that conversation; VAC makes the formal eligibility decision.

Can I use the check if I have already received a VAC decision?

Yes. You can use it if you want to discuss a decision, are considering a review or appeal, or have a condition that has changed since your assessment.

What will the VAC Status Checker tell me?

The checker helps us understand your service, claim history and what you are trying to do so our team can have a more useful follow-up conversation. It does not connect to My VAC Account or retrieve live application updates.
