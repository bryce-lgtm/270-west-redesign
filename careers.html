Careers at 270 West Consulting

Work that makes a complex journey easier.

At 270 West, our team helps Canadian veterans and their families understand and navigate benefits, care and support. We are looking for thoughtful, dependable people who want to make complicated situations clearer and more manageable for others.

View current opportunities →

Meaningful work, built around people.

The work is detailed, personal and practical. One conversation may involve understanding a veteran’s service history. Another may mean coordinating an appointment, organizing information or helping someone understand what happens next.

What connects it all is a clear purpose: helping veterans and their families access the benefits and support they earned through service.

  1. 01

    Meaningful work

    What you do can help make a complicated experience clearer and less burdensome for a veteran or family.

  2. 02

    People, not files

    Every situation is different. We listen first, avoid assumptions and treat each veteran’s circumstances with care and respect.

  3. 03

    A team approach

    Our work often connects Benefit Navigators, health-care professionals, community resources and family members.

  4. 04

    Details matter

    Benefits navigation involves information, appointments, decisions and follow-through. We value people who can manage the details without losing sight of the person behind them.

What working here feels like

Supportive, practical and hands-on. We take the work seriously without making it feel unnecessarily complicated.

People are expected to follow through, communicate clearly, ask questions when something does not make sense and treat veterans, families and colleagues with respect. We work through complexity together and stay focused on what is useful to the person in front of us.

Who thrives here

You do not need to know every answer on day one. You do need to care about getting the details right and about how people experience the work.

  • You listen well

    You make room for someone to explain their circumstances before deciding what they need.

  • You follow through

    You keep commitments, track details and close the loop.

  • You are comfortable with complexity

    You can work through different routes and changing circumstances without making the experience feel complicated for the client.

  • You communicate clearly

    You explain what matters in plain language and know when someone needs context, not jargon.

  • You use good judgement

    You know when to act, when to ask and when another person’s expertise is needed.

  • You treat service with respect

    Every veteran’s experience is different and deserves to be approached without assumptions.

Military experience is valued. It is not required for every role.

270 West was built with veterans at its roots, and lived military experience can bring valuable perspective to our work. At the same time, strong teams need different backgrounds and areas of expertise.

What matters in every role is respect for veterans, a willingness to learn and the ability to bring care, clarity and follow-through to the work.
