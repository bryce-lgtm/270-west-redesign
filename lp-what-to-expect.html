Hands-on support for Veterans Affairs Canada benefits

We help Canadian veterans and their families with disability benefits claims, reviews, appeals and reassessments.

Our services

You may be starting a claim, reviewing a decision or dealing with a condition that has changed. We work with you on the next steps for your situation.

Claims

VAC Claims

We help you prepare your VAC disability benefits application, including a clear description of your condition and how it relates to your service. We coordinate medical appointments with experienced health-care professionals and review the application with you before you file it with VAC.

·Claim and form preparation with you
·Document checklist tailored to your situation
·Review it with you before it is filed
Reviews & Appeals

VAC Reviews & Appeals

If you disagree with a VAC decision, we help you understand the review routes, gather supporting information and identify what may be needed next.

·Decision letter review
·Medical assessment and supporting evidence coordination
·Relevant precedent research and review preparation
Reassessment

VAC Reassessments

We help you decide whether a VAC reassessment is the right next step, connect you with an appropriate medical professional where an updated assessment is needed, and prepare the reassessment paperwork with you.

·Existing assessment review
·Medical information review
·Reassessment information prepared with you
Support

Ongoing Support

Support continues beyond a claim decision. We help veterans and families understand VAC letters and programs, connect with medical and community resources, and navigate other supports that may become relevant over time.

·Decision letter walkthroughs
·Family & caregiver guidance
·Related benefits, treatment supports and community resources

Clear fees. Support from start to finish.

Conversations with our team are always free and never commit you to using our services. If you choose to work with us, our fee is 10% on a successful claim. That fee reflects the full support we provide, from research and preparation to helping you file, coordinating appointments, organizing documentation, tracking what is outstanding, explaining VAC updates and decisions, and helping you understand the benefits and supports available to you.

You only pay if your claim is successful, and we explain the fee and terms clearly before you decide to proceed.

How long it takes

The time needed to prepare a claim depends on your circumstances and the information required. VAC’s review time also varies. We explain the steps within our control and keep you informed when VAC needs something further. We cannot guarantee when VAC will decide a claim or what the decision will be.

270 West Consulting is independent and is not affiliated with Veterans Affairs Canada. VAC makes decisions about eligibility and benefits.
