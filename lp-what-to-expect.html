Who we are

Standing with Canada’s veterans.

We help veterans and their families understand and navigate the disability benefits process: understanding the options, organizing what a claim needs, preparing and filing the documentation, and keeping you informed about what comes next.

We work with mental health professionals and medical clinics across the country so assessments reflect the full scope of your experience. Where a current provider won’t complete VAC paperwork, we can help you find one who will.

Our fee for benefit navigation and filing is 10%, and we only get paid if your claim is successful. There is no fee for an initial conversation, and many of the supports we provide carry no charge at all.
