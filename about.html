Hands-on support through the VAC benefits process

True west. For those who served.

270 West works with Canadian veterans and their families on disability claims, decision reviews and reassessments. We take on practical work throughout the process and help you make informed decisions about your claim.

Founder’s story

One veteran found a way forward. Then he helped the next.

What began with veterans helping veterans has grown into something bigger. But our reason for being has never changed.

Long before 270 West had a name, one of our founders was facing a challenge familiar to many Canadian veterans.

After retiring from the Royal Canadian Navy, he found himself navigating Veterans Affairs Canada (VAC), trying to understand the benefits available for injuries sustained during his military career. He learned the system firsthand, its requirements, its processes and the complexities that can make it difficult to know where to begin.

As he found his way through, he started helping friends and fellow veterans do the same.

One veteran became another. Word spread. Another veteran joined him, followed by his brother. There was no grand business plan. Just a shared belief that what they had learned could make someone else's journey a little easier.

From helping a few to making a difference for many.

As more veterans reached out, what began as informal help grew into something that needed a more lasting foundation.

During a difficult time for the family, around the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic, another family member stepped in. He learned directly from those already doing the work, then spent more than a year developing his own understanding of the VAC system.

Together, they built the foundation for what would become 270 West Consulting.

In those early days, the work wasn't always about preparing claims. Sometimes it meant helping someone clear out their home. Other times, it meant arranging property repairs or finding practical solutions to challenges a veteran was facing.

Those experiences helped shape a philosophy we still believe in today: to truly support someone, you have to understand what they need, not just what form they need to complete.

More than paperwork. People who care.

As 270 West grew, so did our understanding of what meaningful veteran support could look like.

Navigating benefits often means bringing many pieces together: understanding eligibility, gathering medical evidence, connecting with healthcare professionals, preparing documentation and knowing what comes next.

We built a growing team and a network of healthcare providers across Canada to help make that journey clearer, more coordinated and more personal.

Today, our approach is grounded in the same principles that inspired our founders: listen first, explain things clearly, treat every veteran with respect and stay involved throughout the journey.

We understand military life because veterans helped build this company. We know that every person's circumstances are different. And we believe the right support comes from knowledgeable people who take the time to understand those circumstances.

We also believe that better support happens when people work together. That's why we value our relationships with healthcare professionals, veteran organizations and others who share a commitment to serving Canada's veteran community.

Our beginning still guides everything we do.

We started by helping veterans find their way through a complicated system. Today, we have more people, more experience and more resources to offer, but our purpose remains the same: helping veterans and their families access the benefits and care they've earned through service.

We know that every claim represents a person, a family and a life beyond military service.

And whether you're just beginning to explore your options or already navigating the process, we're here to help you understand the path ahead and decide what support is right for you.

Because your service matters. Your story matters. And you deserve to be supported every step of the way.

How we put our
promise into practice

More than advice. Someone in your corner.

Our approach rests on three commitments: taking the time to understand your situation, doing the work that moves your claim forward and helping you reach other support when you need it.

Veteran Understanding

Our roots are in a family of veterans, but we don’t assume that every veteran has the same experience. We listen to your service history, your health concerns and what matters to you before discussing a path forward. We explain the process without rushing you or asking you to fit your story into a standard script.

Active Involvement

We do more than tell you which forms to complete. We help gather and organize relevant information, prepare your application, review it with you and help submit it. If VAC asks for more information, we help you respond. You stay informed, ask questions and approve what goes forward in your name.

Connected Support

A claim can involve more than paperwork. Where it helps, we connect you with medical professionals, community resources and practical support for you or your family. We also help you make sense of decisions and consider what support may be useful afterward.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is 270 West part of Veterans Affairs Canada?

No. We are independent of VAC. We help veterans work through the benefits process, while VAC makes decisions on applications.

Will I still make decisions about my claim?

Yes. We take on preparation, organization and follow-up, but it remains your claim. We review the information with you and seek your approval before it is submitted.

Can my family or caregiver be involved?

Yes, if you want them involved. Partners, family members and caregivers can play an important role in helping you manage appointments, information and decisions.

Does 270 West charge for all its support?

No. Many of the supports and resources we provide are free. Our fee for benefit navigation and filing is 10%, payable only if your claim is successful. Your first conversation is free, and we explain our fees and terms before you decide whether to work with us.
